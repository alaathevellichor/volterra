Alhambra Motif — Francisco Muros Úbeda

A luxurious reproduction of Alhambra Motif, an early 20th-century oil painting by Spanish artist Francisco Muros Úbeda. The artwork captures the magnificent interior of the Alhambra Palace in Granada, featuring elegant Moorish horseshoe arches, intricate geometric tilework, and beautifully detailed muqarnas vaulting.

Inspired by the architectural beauty of Andalusia, this piece brings a timeless sense of history, elegance, and Moorish artistry to any interior. Its rich architectural details and warm tones make it a distinctive statement piece for sophisticated spaces.
